Earn Your Stripes: Privacy Policy

For the Steam demo. Last updated November 9, 2026.

In short: the demo sends game data only if you say yes. The data is anonymous, it helps fix bugs and balance the game, it is never sold, and it is deleted after 90 days. You can change your answer any time in Settings > Gameplay > Send game data.

Who we are

Earn Your Stripes is made by Remo Games, an independent developer (one person, not a company). For any question about this policy or your data, write to remogamesdev@gmail.com.

When data is sent

The first time you start the demo, it asks whether it may send anonymous game data. Nothing is sent unless you choose Yes, send it. If you choose No thanks, the demo sends nothing.

What is sent, if you say yes

  • A random ID made on your computer the first time. It is not linked to your name, email or Steam account. It is shown in Settings as your data ID.
  • The game build you are playing.
  • Your computer's hardware: operating system, processor and its number of cores, memory, graphics card and its memory, screen size and refresh rate, display mode, quality level and system language.
  • Your battles: the map, side, mission, weather and your role; when battles start and end, whether you won, how long you played, your kills and deaths, and what killed you (the cause, the weapon and the distance).
  • Performance: frame rate, how long frames take on the processor and graphics card, long frames, memory use and how many soldiers were in the battle.
  • Error messages from the game. Before they are sent, the names of folders on your computer are removed from them (for example, C:\Users\yourname\... becomes C:\Users\<user>\...).

What is never kept

  • Your name, email address, Steam account or Steam ID.
  • Your IP address. Like any internet connection, the request to our server carries it, but our server does not store it and keeps no access log.
  • Your files, or anything else from your computer beyond the list above.

Why

To find and fix bugs and crashes, to make the game run better on more computers, and to balance the game. We only collect this data with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

Where it is kept, and for how long

  • The data is stored on Amazon Web Services servers in the United States (US West, Northern California). If you live outside the United States, your data is sent there.
  • Each piece of data is deleted automatically 90 days after it arrives.
  • The server's own error log (problems in the server itself, not your game data) is kept for two weeks.
  • A copy of what the demo recorded also stays in the game's save folder on your computer, in a folder named Playtest. You can delete it yourself.

Who sees it

Only Remo Games, to develop Earn Your Stripes. The data is never sold, never used for advertising, and not shared with anyone else. Amazon Web Services stores it for us as our hosting provider.

Your choices and rights

  • Turn it off: Settings > Gameplay > Send game data > Off. The demo stops recording at once and deletes any data still waiting to be sent.
  • See or delete your data: email remogamesdev@gmail.com with your data ID (shown in Settings). We will send you a copy or delete it, as you ask.
  • If you live in the European Union, the United Kingdom or another place with similar laws, you also have the right to complain to your data protection authority.

Children

If you are under 16, please ask a parent or guardian before turning on game data. If you think a child's data was sent without that, email us with the data ID and we will delete it.

Steam

The demo runs through Steam, which collects its own data under Valve's privacy policy. The demo does not send us your Steam account or Steam ID.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will show the new date. If the game starts collecting something new, it will ask you again before sending it.
